Giving instructions & other ways to partner

Choose an option below to see the steps. Your donation is completed on the giving provider’s own website.

🇺🇸 US giving — YWAM Montana Step-by-step
  1. Click Give Support below.
  2. Under Designated For, scroll down and select Lee, Andrew.
  3. Enter the amount you would like to give.
  4. Choose a one-time or monthly recurring donation.
  5. For monthly support, consider creating an account to manage and track donations.
Give through YWAM Montana ↗
🇨🇦 Canada giving — Charitable Impact Step-by-step
  1. Open the Mission Cambodge giving page on Charitable Impact.
  2. Choose Envoyer une fois (one-time) or Envoyer mensuellement (monthly), and enter the amount in CAD.
  3. Complete the email, payment card, and Destinataire du reçu fiscal (tax receipt recipient) details requested on the form.
  4. Review the details and submit on Charitable Impact.
